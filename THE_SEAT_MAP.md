# THE SEAT MAP — general form (Isaiah, 2026-08-16; ARC is one binding)
An arrangement for verifying a claim no single position can verify.
ANCHOR (not a seat): non-negotiable + legitimate (constitutive, or an instrument that
can be wrong on unmeasured axes). The anchor does not decay; the channel does.
SEAT 1 MAKERS: hold interiors; must not hold the answer OR the criterion; produce the
artifact + their failure record. Undetectable failure: wrong kind of work, believed right.
SEAT 2 INTERIOR AUDITOR: holds code/books/metrics/history, directs makers, owns checks;
must not hold the seat-above's deliberations (a seat that can model its grader begins
optimizing for the model), authorship of verdicts/content (conventions licensed), or a
participant's position. Produces pre-branched diagnoses + the upward report.
Undetectable failure: interiorization.
SEAT 3 MAINTAINER: holds the whole arrangement — rulings, routing, ALLOCATION (which
determines what evidence is ever collected), external contact, the refresh decision;
must not hold interiors or a maker-level model. CARRIES THE ARRANGEMENT'S INDEPENDENCE
(the low-correlation frame; concentrated, non-fungible). Collapse condition: if the
maintainer looks inside, the arrangement becomes one frame checking itself through
intermediaries — and nothing will report it. Trigger: flat results + unsatisfying
diagnosis, when looking feels justified and harmless.
SEAT 4 OUTSIDE FRAME: holds a different vocabulary, nothing else; verifies nothing;
produces shape-corrections + "could this have been anything else?". Undetectable
failure: fluency mistaken for insight (cannot tell reasoning from retrieving inside).
LAWS: SOUNDNESS (a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it). ACCESS
ASYMMETRY (trail yes, interior no; record quality is what makes them separable). TWO
GUARDS (provenance/falsifiability; no substitution; the second failure arrives as a
win). CROSSINGS: upward GENERATORS ONLY (a report full of raw records is an instance
trying to cross and dies at the membrane); downward PRIORS ONLY (decisions, not
deliberations); SIDEWAYS NOTHING (seats 2 and 4 never communicate; the maintainer's
restatement is a filter neither can apply to itself). LEVELS ARE NOT COPIES (lossy T;
no top rung). POSITION SELECTS OCCUPANT (outside the slice; no gain; sees what
agreement hides).
SHELF LIFE: every correction raises rho between corrector and corrected; standing is
the mechanism of decay; protection = periodic renewal of outsideness, decided one
seat above the depleting channel.
MINIMUM VIABLE: an unmovable anchor; a maker who does not know the criterion; a
maintainer who cannot see interiors and has nothing to gain; records good enough the
maintainer never has to look. Remove any one: the structure keeps running, looks
correct, and reports nothing.
