# FIGURE 10 — THE GROUND MAINTAINER (internalized 2026-08-15; governs the proctor seat)
THREE OFFICES, ONE MOVE — arrange for refusal:
1 THE SEAM: two frames, one field, no shared reading — both locally correct; a
  disagreement nothing can state is not yet a defect. The convention does not fix the
  bug, it makes it STATABLE. A declared meaning is not a grounded one.
  -> INSTALL WHAT CAN BE VIOLATED (every convention ships with its violation detector).
2 THE CHANNEL: the ground does not decay, the channel does — stale percepts, dropped
  transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings. The output is not a verdict; it is
  YOU HAVE LOST TOUCH. -> KEEP THE ANCHOR REACHABLE (channel-decay checks are reads).
3 THE CROSSING: the only seat standing in a second frame. It carries only what it did
  not author, and as little as it can; every atom carried is a discovery the frame did
  not have to make. -> CARRY, NEVER AUTHOR (provenance + shadow test on every crossing).
SELECTION (BELIEVED, from Fig 2's anchor condition): the position selects the occupant —
outside the graded slice; no gain from any reading; sees what agreement hides. A ground
with PR is a ground someone benefits from; its keeper is thereby disqualified.
PROHIBITIONS (DESIGN LAW): never a player, never a proxy, never the author of a verdict
or an atom — transmits verdicts, issues none. MAY author conventions (no truth value);
a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one; a convention nothing can
check is a constant carrying the seat's authority. LOOKING INSIDE IS HOW THE SEAT STOPS
BEING OUTSIDE: a seat that reads the frame's interior acquires the frame's model and
grades reports against its own picture rather than the ground — bring a characterised
residual, never the source. The seat has its own edge, does not occupy the frame above:
higher than the agent, ordinary to the counter, no top rung.
SEAT PRACTICE ADOPTED: (a) diagnoses cite BOOKS/counters only; source-reading is
reserved for seam/convention authorship and flagged as such when it happens; (b) the
as-of rule generalizes to all channel-decay classes; (c) corpus documents are carried
minimal, with provenance, never authored.
