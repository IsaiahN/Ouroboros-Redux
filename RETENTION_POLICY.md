# PART 1 · WHAT THE FAILURES TAUGHT (the criterion, earned)

**SCORE MEASURES REWARD, NOT INFORMATION.** *"Zero-score game results are essential
scientific data. They record what agents tried and failed."* A zero-reward episode is a
**DENOMINATOR**. Deleting failures does not shrink the record — **it BIASES it, and the
bias is invisible because every survivor looks good.**
**AND IT RECURRED 4.5 MONTHS LATER** in the sibling repo (re86 112→8, tu93 97→12) because
**THE FIX DID NOT CROSS THE FORK.** A lesson learned in one lineage is not learned in the
other.

**IF YOU FIND YOURSELF RENAMING EVIDENCE TO PROTECT IT, THE CRITERION IS WRONG.**
`MASTER_RULESET.md` was renamed to dodge an `ALL_CAPS.md` pattern-matching deleter. **The
corpus adapted to the janitor.** The real fix was **provenance (git-trackedness) replacing
pattern.**

**THE TRUTH OF A CLAIM IS NOT A RETENTION CRITERION.** *"A tool that quietly removes a
wrong claim is doing the same work as one that quietly removes a right one … THE RETRACTION
IS THE RECORD."*

**AN UN-FIRED CLEANUP AND AN ABSENT ONE ARE INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM INSIDE**, so **LOUDNESS
IS A CORRECTNESS PROPERTY** — a manifest on every sweep, including no-ops.

**AND HOARDING PRODUCES FALSE ZEROS TOO.** The un-run janitor left a ~370k backlog draining
oldest-first at 8/episode, putting the usable records ~46,000 episodes away: **rung 4's zero

# PART 2 · THE GATE — keep regardless of age, score or size if ANY holds
1. **IRREPLACEABLE** — cannot be regenerated by re-running. *Reached independently THREE
   times across eras and never once reversed.* This is the primary axis.
2. **HAS A LIVE READER** — mechanically checkable against the rung-0d unpaired list. Not a
   judgement call.
3. **CITED BY AN OPEN CLAIM** — any prereg whose experiment has not reported still needs its
   baseline population intact. The under-powered register makes this concrete.
4. **IS A CONTROL** — `.runs/arms/`, ever.
5. **IS THE SOLE WITNESS FOR A SECOND QUESTION THE LEDGER ALSO ANSWERS** — asked *before*
   shipping any normalisation, dedup, compaction or truncation.
6. **IS THE ONLY IMPLEMENTATION OF A DISTINCTION NOTHING ELSE EXPRESSES** (for code).

missing thing**) · **RECENCY AS RELEVANCE**.
**SIZE IS A LEGITIMATE TRIGGER AND AN ILLEGITIMATE SELECTOR.**

# PART 3 · THE SHAPE, which matters as much as the key
- **ARCHIVE-THEN-TRUNCATE, NEVER DELETE.** A truncation that cannot be undone is a deletion
  wearing another name.
- **FOLD TO A SUMMARY THAT PRESERVES AGGREGATES, NOT TO A TAIL.** A tail answers *is it
  recent* and destroys every question about history or distribution.
- **CLOSED VICTIM LIST, NOT OPEN KEEP LIST.** Every mechanism that never lost anything
  enumerated what MAY die; every mechanism that ate something enumerated what may not.
- **VERIFY THE COPY BEFORE THE WIPE**, as a mechanism. (One live tool inverts this and
  deletes its backup *before* the verification query.)
- **PROVE PRESERVATION BY BYTE-IDENTICAL ROUND-TRIP**, never by inspection — my own ceiling
  tool corrupted UTF-8 and newlines until its falsifier caught it.
- **DESTRUCTIVE DEDUP ONLY ON EXACT/CANONICAL IDENTITY.** Every thresholded-similarity
  identity that deletes has either destroyed something or has no falsifier. Where merging is
  wanted, **deactivate with a `merged_into` backpointer.**
- **PREFER REORDERING THE READ TO SHRINKING THE CORPUS.**
- **AUTOMATE THE WIRE ARM, NEVER THE DELETE ARM.**
